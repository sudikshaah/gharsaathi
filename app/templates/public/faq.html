Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, matching, and work verification on Sahayak.

Domestic helpers upload identification documents (Aadhaar cards) and Police Clearance Certificates (PCC). Our administrators audit these documents manually in our admin queue. Once approved, helper profiles receive verified badges which award them matching priority scores.

Our match engine calculates compatibility scores dynamically based on: Proximity distance (30%), salary expectations alignment (25%), skills intersections (20%), minimum experience requirement checks (15%), and helper document verification status bonus (10%).

Proximity matching calculates the great-circle distance between coordinates of the household employer and the worker. Compatible matches receive a full score if the distance is under 1 km, which decreases linearly to 0% at 5 km or more to encourage hyper-local, walkable employment.

Users can report disputes (abuse, continuous absences, theft, or payment delays) against profiles. Tickets are routed to our administrative complaints dashboard where administrators investigate and can suspend user accounts if terms of service are breached.
